# Public vanilla-LIF validation

The additional public vanilla-LIF test is complete for all 10,000 canonical CIFAR-10 test images. The authors' [OTTT-SNN NeurIPS2022 checkpoint](https://github.com/pkuxmq/OTTT-SNN), pinned source `c15d5da05eea0c48e1fa837a4f8073297006d93f`, uses VGG11 weight-standardized synapses, eight OnlineLIF layers, T=6, tau=2, threshold1 with equality firing and immediate subtractive reset. We used its trained weights and original normalization without retraining or parameter/range tuning.

Original FP32 inference gives 9,349/10,000 correct (93.49%). The declared integer neuron with actual exact packed leakage feedback gives 9,345/10,000 (93.45%), a conversion difference of −0.04 percentage points, with 104 changed predictions. Synapses and readout remain FP32. The authors' checkpoint metadata max accuracy0.9358 is retained separately and is not our reproduced result. Integer conversion rounds effective currents nearest-even to unit1/256, leaks by ceil(U/2), fires on charge>=256 and subtracts256 immediately. Packing does not alter this declared equation.

All 24,576,000,000 actual leakage updates matched an independent scalar ceil oracle before their decoded results fed the membrane update. Earlier separate plain-integer and packed executions of the first64 held-out images compare every membrane and spike in all layers/timesteps, with zero mismatches and all logits bit identical. The full run uses original FP32 plus actual packed-integer feedback, with the per-update oracle, rather than redundantly running a third model. This distinction remains explicit in `benchmark_acceptance.json`.

All actual old leakage operands fit [−11,820,11,815], and every stored state after threshold/reset fits [−12,011,12,006]. Old/current/stored min/max are recorded exhaustively by layer, including zero-bypassed updates. Signed16 covers this complete run without changing units, coefficients or weights: old operand exceptions are0; stored-state exceptions are provably0 from the exhaustive min/max. Original signed12 would reject289,441,562 nonzero old operands, about1.416% of the nonzero workload, so the fixed16 extension is necessary for faithful hardware applicability. No clipping or favorable rescaling is used.

Common zero bypass removes4,135,028,887 updates. Of20,440,971,113 nonzero operations,10,952,413,622 fit signed9. Greedy ordered adaptive pairing forms4,604,882,418 pairs and requires15,836,088,695 issue slots; the exact fixed two-lane schedule requires10,220,498,016. Thus adaptation increases issue slots54.944%, while useful operations per adaptive DSP issue are1.291. These are operation schedule counts, not accelerator throughput or latency.

This is an accepted negative hardware-applicability result. Tau2 is implemented exactly by ceil(U/2), an arithmetic shift plus rounding bit, requiring zero DSPs. Higher DSP utilization does not demonstrate an advantage against this specialized control. The broad LIF evidence therefore limits, rather than inflates, the generality claim: a general coefficient engine must justify its advantage on the declared heterogeneous/runtime-coefficient operation, alongside strong constant controls for models that permit them. It does not establish superiority over the FireFly accelerators simply by sharing CIFAR-10.
