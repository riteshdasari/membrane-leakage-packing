# Method-level prior-work audit of E5

Audit date: 10 October 2026. Target: `sequential_paper_trial/leak_core.sv`, E5, exact `ceil(beta*U/256)` for signed12 U and unsigned beta0..256. This is a bounded literature and released-source audit, not proof that no equivalent implementation exists.

## Decision

Retain the narrow, application-specific claim. Do **not** claim that actual-value narrowness detection, adaptive operation packing, DSP multiplication packing, modulo spill reconstruction, or exact signed packing is new. The full methods checked here have not revealed the exact conjunction of an actual-membrane signed9 pair admission, same-coefficient test, signed12 single fallback, and this exact ceil-leakage kernel. Whether that combination supplies sufficient novelty is a reviewer judgment; it cannot be settled by a keyword absence.

The two initially abstract-only gaps are no longer equally open. UDP's complete released multiplication module has now been inspected and pinned. Its full ACM paper remains inaccessible in this environment. The six-signed ICFPT paper's method remains inaccessible. Therefore **a blanket claim that final prior-work equivalence checking is complete would be false**.

## Candidate, inspected directly

E5 examines two consecutive values. It consumes two only when both values sign-extend from nine bits, their coefficients match, and two operands are available. Otherwise it consumes one full-width operand. Stored state, state unit and operand order remain unchanged. Its packed input has stride16 and uses 25 bits. A modulo-two reference recovers the lower borrow; a boundary correction repairs the higher extracted quotient. Its declared outputs are rounded leakage values, not the complete unrounded products.

This is numerical range adaptation driven by actual membrane values. It does not predict spike activity, vary stored membrane precision, scale state units, or offer exactness relative to the original floating-point network. Exactness is relative to the declared integer update.

## Full primary methods checked

**DSP-Packing, Sommer et al., FPL2022.** [Full primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.11028). Sections IV–VI generalize arbitrary-width packing and analyze signed-product contamination, full correction, approximation and overpacking. Section VI-B reconstructs contaminating product bits using cheap low-bit Boolean products; Section VII addresses packed SNN additions. E5 uses these established arithmetic principles. The inspected method does not choose pair versus scalar leakage from actual membrane range. Its SNN discussion is addition packing, and its overpacking evaluation permits approximation. This establishes substantial arithmetic ancestry, not an exact E5 match.

**Arithmetic Packing, Bornträger et al., 2026.** [Full primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.11065). Sections III-B/C use the DSP pre-adder for signed packing and modulo-four references for exact lane spill tracking. Section III-D separates low DSP and high fabric accumulation portions. These are close predecessors of E5's modulo-two correction, although E5 has a single rounded leakage product rather than continued MAC accumulation. The demonstrated precision/layout choices are configured for MatVec/convolution. Section V leaves workload-adaptive packing in real time as future work. E5's correction is a specialization of established spill reconstruction, with a smaller reference because its bounded borrow has only two possibilities.

**XtraMAC, Yu et al., 2026.** [Full primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06052). Sections III-C and IV provide datatype-dependent packing through a shared integer mantissa path. Runtime format selection controls mapping and accumulation; application integration supplies a per-tile datatype selector. This is already adaptive DSP packing. The inspected work supports INT/FP and mixed datatypes for LLMs and does not describe the E5 membrane-range pair/full policy. Its runtime adaptation changes the chosen datatype interpretation; E5 retains a single stored integer contract and changes only how many legal operands share one issue.

**Brooks and Martonosi, TOCS2000.** [Author-hosted full paper](https://mrmgroup.cs.princeton.edu/papers/dbrooks-tocs2000.pdf), DOI [10.1145/350853.350856](https://doi.org/10.1145/350853.350856). Sections 5.1–5.2 detect actual narrow operand values at issue time and pack operations of the same kind into one ALU, retaining the normal full-width execution case. This is direct conceptual prior art for value-driven admission, even though the study packs arithmetic/logic/shifts rather than DSP multiplications and does not implement SNN leakage. Section 6 also considers speculative packing with overflow replay. Merely naming U a membrane cannot make the underlying actual-value policy new.

**UDP, Wu et al., FPGA2026.** [Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1145/3748173.3779194), [author source](https://github.com/wintermelon008/UDP). Released source revision `f5a6b4f2522e54a815f5026b67cf7f78b7438160`; downloaded public archive is retained here. `src/main/scala/UDP.scala` is a complete signed multiplication module with widths and parallelism specified as Chisel elaboration parameters. Runtime operand signs determine correction terms; the exported interface has operand vectors and valid signals, with no range-selected lane count or wide fallback. The included test fixes 4-bit operands and a 3-by-2 layout. Therefore that released module does not implement E5's policy. This source observation is narrower than a claim about every architecture in the inaccessible full paper.

**SkipOPU, 2026.** [Full primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.14785), Section 3.2. Its shared-operand FP16 mantissa packing truncates selected operand bits and restores their arithmetic contribution through DSP pre-adder/C-port manipulation. This is another exact information-restoration predecessor. Its dynamic computation allocation concerns LLM attention pruning, and its inspected packing layout is not an actual-U leakage admission policy. It further rules out claiming generic exact truncation restoration as new.

## What can actually distinguish the present kernel

The useful specialization is that the caller requests a quotient with declared ceil rounding, not full products or a MAC reduction. For admitted operands `U0,U1`, define `q0=beta*U0+255`, so `-65281 <= q0 <= 65535`. Relative to a 16-bit field the lower term can contribute only a borrow of zero or minus one; it cannot carry positively. A one-bit modulo reference therefore suffices to recover that contribution. Nine signed bits suffice for each declared leakage result, including beta256.

Thus stride16 plus a signed9 upper operand fits a 25-bit input on DSP48E1. A straightforward layout retaining two complete generic 9-by-9 products at stride18 needs 27 input bits. This direct algebra explains why tailoring a general packing technique to an exact rounded kernel can matter. It is **not** a proof that no prior rounded-product packing method used the same trick. The measured area/issue/energy trade-off must accompany this specialization; application naming alone is weak novelty.

For completeness, let `A=(U0+256)+(U1<<16)` and `C=255-256*beta+(255<<16)`. Then `P=A*beta+C=q0+((beta*U1+255)<<16)`. With `n=(q0<0)`, the upper field is `T=beta*U1+255-n`. Its parity equals `((beta&1)&(U1&1)) XOR 1 XOR n`, giving the RTL's one-bit borrow reconstruction. The upper quotient needs one increment precisely when `n=1` and `T mod256=255`. The lower quotient is its eight extracted bits minus `256*n`. This derives the implemented corrections without invoking a generic new overflow-tracking invention.

Recommended claim: **an exact membrane-range-selected pair/full leakage primitive with fixed state precision, a proven rounded-output contract, and measured implementation trade-offs on public SNN traces**. Avoid first-ever claims. Also avoid universal superiority: identity or power-of-two leakage has a much cheaper shift/wire control, while all-wide bursts favor the fixed full-domain pair implementation.

## FireFly validation targets, taken from primary papers

**Original FireFly** [Sec.V-C/TableIII](https://arxiv.org/html/2301.01905v5): SCNN5 on MNIST; SCNN7 on CIFAR10; SCNN11 on CIFAR100; SCNN9 on DVS-CIFAR10 and DVS-Gesture. The stated training setup uses four time steps, threshold0.5 and LIF tau2. Hardware supports IF/LIF and approximates leakage with a shift. Dataset or topology alignment alone is not reproduction of its checkpoint, quantization or arithmetic.

**FireFly v2** [Sec.VI/TablesIV–VI](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.16158): the original SCNN benchmark family, plus CIFAR-Net on CIFAR10/CIFAR100 at four steps and SEW-ResNet34 on ImageNet at eight steps. Its neurodynamics unit supports IF/LIF/residual-membrane neurons. The paper does not explicitly associate every table row with a complete neuron equation; do not guess that every model uses nontrivial multiplication leakage.

**FireFly-S** [Sec.VI-A/TableII](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.15578v1): SCNN5 on MNIST, SCNN7 on DVS-Gesture, SCNN9 on CIFAR10, with sparse four-bit quantization. Its hardware discusses IF/LIF. Its topology/training and pruning differ from the earlier FireFly models, so shared SCNN numbering is not checkpoint identity. The model descriptions are relevant targets but do not imply public checkpoints were reproduced.

**FireFly-T**, an additional newer series member, [Sec.V-A](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.12771): CIFAR-Net and Spikingformer-4-256 on CIFAR10, Spikingformer-8-512 on ImageNet, using BrainCog and four-bit quantization. This strengthens the case for a future spiking-transformer validation but does not authorize relabeling a different model as FireFly-T.

A scientifically useful validation set therefore includes a public vanilla LIF classification workload and a nontrivial/learned-leakage workload. The former can be a negative applicability control when its actual leakage is a shift. Report the original coefficient, reset, input-decay, rounding and time steps for every model. Do not replace tau2 with an expensive coefficient to favor the DSP primitive. AdEx's extra nonlinear/adaptation terms require their own contract and are not validated by testing this leakage suboperation.
